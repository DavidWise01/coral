# CORAL · CRL

> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **LIFE SCIENCE**.

CORAL (CRL) — the partnership that builds a world: two lives fused — the coral polyp (a colonial cnidarian animal) + zooxanthellae (photosynthetic algae in its flesh that hand it ~90% of its food) — repeated into reefs, the largest living structures on Earth, sheltering ~a quarter of marine life. Two threads at once: emergence by merger (like lichen) AND a colony of clones (like the man o' war). BLEACHING = the symbiosis breaking under heat: the coral expels its algae, whitens, and starves — the clearest living signature of a warming sea. Honest about wonder AND the wound; 'super coral'/assisted evolution flagged as effort, not rescue. Vellum/purple + pulsing aeon favicon; LIVE symbiosis-and-bleaching interactive. Ruth Gates (d.2018) minted, living researchers cited. 11 emergents.
